Meditation Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Meditation Lab beta

Meditation Lab is a journal you can keep alone or share with a few people in a circle. This page says what we keep, who else handles it, and what happens when you delete things. Meditation Lab is run by Korey Mauer ("we"), who you can reach at support@meditationlab.co.

The short version

We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. To name and sort what you write, safety-check what you share, and turn your voice into text, Meditation Lab sends it to two AI services: Claude, run by Anthropic, and OpenAI. Entries you mark Private are never sent to any AI. You can delete any entry, or your whole account, in the app.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: email, first and last name, a hashed password, time zone and settings.
  • What you write: your entries and their titles, and what the AI writes about them (the day's name and recap, which circles an entry fits, and the pages and summaries some features build for you).
  • Voice notes: the text, including the unedited transcript, and how long the note was. We don't keep the recording: it is sent to be transcribed and then discarded. If sending fails, the recording stays on your phone until you retry or discard it.
  • Circles: which circles you're in, what you share there, comments on posts, and direct messages between members.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, and the result of the automatic safety check on what you share.
  • Notifications: a push token, if you allow notifications.
  • Crash reports, scrubbed of anything you write.
  • Usage analytics: which screens and buttons are used, linked to your account but never containing your writing, titles or circle names. On by default; you can turn it off in Settings ("Share usage analytics").

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. Drafts you haven't sent stay on your phone, including after you sign out, and the app keeps a copy of recent pages on your phone until you sign out.

2. The AI

  • Claude (Anthropic) names your entries, writes your day's name and recap, tidies up voice-note transcripts, decides which circles an entry fits, writes circle posts and prompts, builds pages written for you, and safety-checks what you share in circles.
  • OpenAI turns your voice into text. Some features also turn entries into embeddings — lists of numbers the app uses to find related entries.

The text of a non-private entry is sent when you save it, and again when you edit it or when a feature that uses it runs (a recap, a page, a circle post). Entries you mark Private are never sent to any AI. Speak turns your voice into text as you talk, so choose Private before you start speaking.

Neither service trains its models on what we send. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days, or keeps it up to two years if its safety systems flag it. OpenAI keeps nothing from transcription and keeps embedding requests for 30 days for abuse monitoring.

You agree to this when you accept the terms; the terms screen names both services.

3. Circles, comments and messages

  • Circles see only what you share with them. Most circles show an AI-written post drawn from your entries. If you turn on Include my own words for a circle, it gets your whole entry. In a prompt circle, your answer is shared as you wrote it, after a safety check.
  • Comments are seen by the circle's members. They're checked automatically after they're posted, and a flagged comment is held back until you decide.
  • Direct messages are seen only by you and the person you're writing to. They're stored on our servers and aren't end-to-end encrypted. They're never checked automatically or sent to any AI.
  • You can take back a post at any time; people who already saw it may have read it. Once you take it back, its comments can't be seen by anyone. Deleting a comment hides it for everyone. Deleting a direct message removes your copy; the other person keeps theirs.
  • Anyone can report a post, comment or message, or block another person. We review reports within 24 hours.

When we read what you wrote: only when someone reports it. A report shows us the reported message and the five before it in that conversation. Our encrypted backups (section 4) hold everything, Private entries included, and are opened only to restore the service after a failure.

4. Deleting things

  • An entry: gone from the app at once. Its text is erased from our database 30 days later. A prompt-circle answer keeps its text until you take the post back.
  • Your account (Settings → Account → Delete account): removes your account, your entries, your Life Circle posts and their comments, and every direct-message conversation you were in, for both people. What you shared in question and prompt circles, and your comments, stay in those circles under your first name.
  • Backups: we keep encrypted copies of the database, the last ten, so we can recover from a failure. A deleted entry leaves the backups as newer copies replace older ones.

5. Services we use

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in (US).
  • Anthropic: the Claude AI service.
  • OpenAI: voice transcription and embeddings.
  • Resend: sign-up and password emails.
  • Expo and Apple: app updates and notifications (a notification can show a circle's name and a member's first name).
  • Sentry: crash reports, scrubbed of your writing.
  • PostHog: usage analytics (US), unless you turn it off.
  • Apple TestFlight: beta distribution.

Your data may be processed in the US and other countries. We share it with these services only to run the app.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database rules keep each account's data separate. Your sign-in is stored in the iOS Keychain, and you can lock the app with Face ID.

7. Your rights and age

You can delete entries and your account in the app, and ask for a copy of your data by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you can also complain to a data-protection authority.

Meditation Lab is for people 18 and over.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact: support@meditationlab.co
